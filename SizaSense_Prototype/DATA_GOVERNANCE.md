# Data governance and safeguarding baseline
This is a design baseline, not legal compliance certification.

- Do not collect names, phone numbers, pregnancy status, diagnoses or identifiable patient records in the public prototype.
- Prefer facility/community-area indicators over individual location traces.
- Label each record as community-reported, partner-submitted, field-verified or laboratory-verified.
- Do not treat unverified reports as proof of pollution, contamination or service failure.
- Risk scores are prioritisation aids, not automated decisions.
- Establish escalation, correction, deletion and complaint routes before a real pilot.
- For work involving children, obtain safeguarding advice, permissions and age-appropriate assent/consent.
- Before deployment: HTTPS, secrets management, role-based access, audit logs, backups, retention controls, privacy impact assessment and threat model.
